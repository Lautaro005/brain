What's in the drawer

brain keeps five kinds of cards. Your agents read them before they answer and write new ones as they learn.

Tell it who you are, once

Your name, what you do, how you like to work. Every agent you connect reads it before answering, so you stop introducing yourself.

Bring the memory you already have

Copy one prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, paste the answer into brain, and everything they remember about you becomes cards, sorted by topic. From then on your agents add new facts on their own.

Yours, on your computer

Where your memory livesLocal
Everything brain knows about you is a folder on your computer: ~/.brain. Nothing is uploaded, and no account exists to sign in to.
Rules of the drawer7
  1. 7.1
    Runs on your machineSearch is powered by a small model that runs locally. brain only goes online to save a page you ask for, to reach the connections you add, to check GitHub for a new version and, only if you turn on remote access, to keep a Cloudflare tunnel open for your cloud agents.
  2. 7.2
    Every change can be undoneEach edit is kept as a version, so an agent can never destroy what you wrote.
  3. 7.3
    Agents stay inside the drawerThey can read and write your brain folder, and nothing else on your computer.
  4. 7.4
    Source availableMIT with the Commons Clause. Read every line on GitHub, change it, make it yours. Just don't sell it.
